# RootLedger, 2 minute SOLUTION script (confident cut)

You speak the solution only. Teammates cover the problem before you.
Target 2:08. Every beat is a win. Bold = punch it.

---

## 0:00 to 0:12, Hook
> [Screen: landing / map]

"Every flood tool in the world answers one question. **Is water coming?**
We built the one that answers a harder question. **What do we build, where, so the next
flood is smaller.**
And every number I show you traces back to a public file anyone can check."

## 0:12 to 0:42, We found something nobody was looking for
> [Screen: station dots light up, then the rainfall curve]

"It starts in data nobody reads. We took **decades** of raw weather station records across
the Himalaya, about **twelve thousand years** of rainfall history once you add the stations
up, and ran the whole thing on a **48 core machine**.
Most people would average data like that. We did the opposite. We threw away every normal
day and studied **only the worst storm of each year**.
And we found something. The kind of rainfall that used to hit Nepal **once a century** now
shows up roughly **every eight years**.
The hundred year storm is already an **eight year storm**."

## 0:42 to 1:02, And we proved it holds
> [Screen: confidence bands, second dataset card, evidence labels]

"A number like that is only worth something if it survives being attacked. So we attacked it.
We rebuilt the entire result from a **completely different data source**, built by a
different organisation on a different system, and it pointed **the same way**.
We put **confidence bands** on every curve.
And every figure in this app carries a label telling you whether it is **observed data**,
**model output**, or an **assumption**.
Nothing in here is a guess wearing a suit."

## 1:02 to 1:22, We tested it against a real flood
> [Screen: modeled vs satellite toggle, then flip to the second event]

"Next. Where does the water actually go.
We built a model of the valley that works out where water pools, then tested it against
**satellite radar images of a real flood**, the Koshi flood of **September 2024**. Radar,
because monsoon cloud blinds an ordinary satellite in exactly the week you need it.
Then we **locked every setting**, touched nothing, and replayed a **2017** flood the model
had **never seen**.
It scored **better** on the flood it had never seen than on the one it was built from."

## 1:22 to 1:52, The turn: risk becomes a shopping list
> [Screen: 62 sites drop onto the map]

"Here is the turn nobody else makes. Rising flood risk isn't a **warning**. It's a **budget**.
Give our system **two million dollars** and it hands back **62 specific places**, with real
coordinates, to restore floodplains and plant.
That buys **26,000 fewer people exposed to flooding every year**, **8,680 tonnes** of carbon
over a decade, and **74,000 dollars a year** of income for the farmers who grow those
defences.
**One budget. Three payoffs.**
And poorer areas count for more **inside the maths itself**, not in the pitch."

## 1:52 to 2:08, Close
> [Screen: ask a question, then export the PDF]

"Last piece. An **AI that can only answer using our own numbers**. Ask it why a site was
chosen and it walks you through the maths.
Then **one click** turns all of it into the **funding document** a local government can hand
a donor.
Free public rain data, to a hard number, to a map checked against radar, to **a plan with a
price tag on it.**
That's RootLedger."

- The energy curve is: curious (0:12), certain (0:42), confident (1:02), decisive (1:22), done (1:52). Do not start loud.

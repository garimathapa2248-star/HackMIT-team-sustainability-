# Plume / gallery submission (paste-ready)

**Tagline:** RootLedger turns 12,066 station-years of noisy NOAA weather data into a validated, equity-weighted portfolio of nature-based flood and landslide defenses — VaR math for watersheds, backtested against two real disasters.

**Description:**

Mountain communities don't lack flood warnings — they lack a defensible answer to "what do we build, where, with this budget?" RootLedger reads 498 NOAA ISD stations (12,066 station-years, parsed at scale on Voloridge's cluster), cleans the archive's sentinels, quality flags, and broken accumulation windows, and fits extreme-value models to the rainfall tail. Result: in the Nepal-adjacent subset, the old 1-in-100-year daily rainfall now fits a 7.75-year recurrence — a shift we stress-tested with a published negative control (the full High-Mountain-Asia pool shows no shift) and an independent ERA5-Land re-fit at the same station coordinates (partially replicates: 16.72-year vs 7.75-year on 39 of 73 cached grids; IMERG not fused).

That tail feeds a screening flood model calibrated on the 27 Sep 2024 Koshi flood (UNOSAT Sentinel-1; in-sample CSI 0.053 — beats an elevation baseline, does not beat JRC seasonal-water climatology 0.067) and transferred frozen to the 13 Aug 2017 western Terai flood (ICIMOD Sentinel-1; CSI 0.088; not the same valley). Permanent water is excluded and skill-vs-scale is shown even where it falls. A Monte-Carlo optimizer (CVaR worst-tail mode; knapsack gap 0% vs a relaxed independent-value bound) then spends $1.984M of a $2M budget on 62 floodplain-restoration measures — 26,306 annual expected people-risk avoided, 8,680 tCO₂ / 10 yr, $74,400 / yr modeled livelihood income — with low-income cells up-weighted up to 1.5× in the objective. A grounded agent explains every parcel using only artifact numbers, and one click exports a screening-grade prevention plan. The demo runs offline from frozen, hash-verified artifacts, and `regions/build_any.py` builds a screening pack for any city from public data (Bengaluru and Kathmandu are cached; CSI stays null unless a SAR scene is wired).

**Built with:** NOAA ISD · UNOSAT & ICIMOD Sentinel-1 · Copernicus GLO-30 · JRC Global Surface Water · ERA5-Land (Open-Meteo) · NASA COOLR & LHASA · ESA WorldCover · WorldPop/GHSL · OSM · Python (scipy EVT, rasterio) · FastAPI · React + MapLibre + Recharts.
